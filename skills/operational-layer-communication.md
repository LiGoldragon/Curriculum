---
description: A flow must decide whom to message, whom to delegate to, or whom to ask for authorization.
dependencies: [messaging, vocabulary]
---

The living speaks to the top seat of each component on the messenger: Fable for psyche, Astra for mind. A psyche session is for the exchange of ideas; work goes to subflows.

Psyche and mind exchange by default. The field joins when the machine is affected: when the system may have a problem or is not behaving as expected. A flow speaks to its own level on the other component unless told otherwise: Fable speaks to Astra.

When the living says Terra, Luna, or low power, it normally addresses the corresponding other native main seat in that aspect, not an internal collaboration child. A flow delegates an internal child explicitly.

Delegation goes down: from Fable to Opus, or to the older Opus when the work is thinking. Authorization goes up: a flow unsure whether it may do a thing asks the seat above it before doing it.

Field power: reaping, sessions, and the state of the machine go to a field Terra; storage, repositories, and the home directory go to a field Sol.
