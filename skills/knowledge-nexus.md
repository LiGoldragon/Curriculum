Four nexuses run today: orchestrate-nexus 0.35.0 (the lock Nexus), two flow-nexus (0.12.2 from the nix profile under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/flow/`, and 0.17.4 as next under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/flow-next/flow/`), message-nexus 0.17.0 under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/message-next/message/`, and lojix-nexus 8.1.0 under `/run/lojix/`. Beside them the older message-daemon 0.14.0 still serves `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/message/`. Only orchestrate is reached by every flow; its sockets are `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/orchestrate-nexus/orchestrate.sock` and, on the live host, `meta-orchestrate.sock`, a name its pre-0.34.0 store saved and resumes, while 0.34.0 and later default to `orchestrate-meta.sock`, which the installed `orchestrate-meta` wrapper targets; the live meta CLI is therefore unreachable until the store is reconfigured and the Nexus restarted. The store is `$XDG_STATE_HOME/orchestrate-nexus/orchestrate-nexus.sema`; a store records its socket paths, and the Nexus refuses a carried store, so a sandbox starts fresh under its own `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` and `XDG_STATE_HOME`. The client reads `ORCHESTRATE_SOCKET` and `ORCHESTRATE_META_SOCKET`; the installed wrappers set them from `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` and ignore a caller's value.

Datom strings on the orchestrate wire are in guillemets; a reason in curly or ASCII quotes splits into words and is refused with `Unreadable.Error.{ Composition [ 1 ] Arity.{ … } }`. The ordinary socket's FlowId is still claimed by the caller; peer-credential caller resolution exists only in flow (ResolveCaller).

Wire: the running orchestrate-nexus 0.35.0 pins signal-orchestrate 3.0.2 and meta-signal-orchestrate 3.0.2 on the old wire (signal 3.0.2, signal-frame 0.3.1, sema-engine 0.15.1). On main, orchestrate 0.36.1 pins signal-orchestrate and meta-signal-orchestrate 4.0.0 on signal 7.0.0 with sema-engine 0.17.0, and signal-ethos-zero and meta-signal-ethos-zero 1.0.0, which nothing serves, sit on signal 7.0.0 with no signal-frame; no running Nexus speaks signal 7.0.0. flow-nexus 0.17.4 and message-nexus 0.17.0 run what is now on main: flow 0.18.0 still pins signal-flow 7.0.0 (rev 1c9e4b3) though signal-flow main is 7.1.0, with meta-signal-flow 11.0.0; message 0.17.1 pins signal-message 8.0.0 and meta-signal-message 0.8.0; all of these are on signal 5.0.0. flow and message pin sema-engine 0.16.0, whose read plans answer UnsupportedReadPlan and whose version stamp is not atomic; sema-engine 0.17.0 executes the Filter read plan and lands an open as one write transaction, and main is 0.18.0, adding Memorable. Every flow, message and orchestrate contract, and orchestrate's own clients, generate with ethos-zero 13.0.0 and protos 0.31.0, and orchestrate-test and flow-test inherit those pins through their flake inputs; only the ethos-zero contracts are on ethos-zero 15.0.0. `nexus` 0.5.0 is a shared lifecycle-types library, not a template. messenger-clj is a Clojure CLI writing to Herdr directly, without a Nexus of its own; it reserves through the orchestrate CLI.

The orchestrate repository's `crates/orchestrate-nexus/tests/live_nexus.rs` runs a Nexus and both CLIs on temporary sockets with a fresh store; it is the pattern for every sandbox check.
