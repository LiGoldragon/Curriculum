Four nexuses run today: orchestrate-nexus 0.35.0 (the lock Nexus), two flow-nexus (0.12.2 from the nix profile under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/flow/`, and 0.17.4 as next under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/flow-next/flow/`), message-nexus 0.17.0 under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/message-next/message/`, and lojix-nexus 8.1.0 under `/run/lojix/`. Beside them the older message-daemon 0.14.0 still serves `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/message/`. Only orchestrate is reached by every flow; its sockets are `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/orchestrate-nexus/orchestrate.sock` and, on the live host, `meta-orchestrate.sock`, a name its pre-0.34.0 store saved and resumes, while 0.34.0 and later default to `orchestrate-meta.sock`, which the installed `orchestrate-meta` wrapper targets; the live meta CLI is therefore unreachable until the store is reconfigured and the Nexus restarted. The store is `$XDG_STATE_HOME/orchestrate-nexus/orchestrate-nexus.sema`; a store records its socket paths, and the Nexus refuses a carried store, so a sandbox starts fresh under its own `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` and `XDG_STATE_HOME`. The client reads `ORCHESTRATE_SOCKET` and `ORCHESTRATE_META_SOCKET`; the installed wrappers set them from `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` and ignore a caller's value.

Datom strings on the orchestrate wire are in guillemets; a reason in curly or ASCII quotes splits into words and is refused with `Unreadable.Error.{ Composition [ 1 ] Arity.{ … } }`. The ordinary socket's FlowId is still claimed by the caller; peer-credential caller resolution exists only in flow (ResolveCaller).

Wire: the running orchestrate-nexus 0.35.0 pins signal-orchestrate 3.0.2 and meta-signal-orchestrate 3.0.2 on the old wire (signal 3.0.2, signal-frame 0.3.1, sema-engine 0.15.1); flow-nexus 0.17.4 pins signal-flow 7.0.0 and meta-signal-flow 11.0.0, and message-nexus 0.17.0 signal-message 8.0.0 and meta-signal-message 0.8.0, on signal 5.0.0. No running Nexus speaks main's wire: on main orchestrate 0.37.0, flow 0.22.0, message 0.19.0 and lojix 9.0.0, their contracts (signal-orchestrate and meta-signal-orchestrate 5.0.0, signal-flow 10.0.0, meta-signal-flow 14.0.0, signal-message 10.0.0, meta-signal-message 0.10.0, signal-lojix 7.0.0 and meta-signal-lojix 8.0.0 on horizon-lib 0.14.0), and signal-ethos-zero and meta-signal-ethos-zero 2.0.0, which nothing serves, all share signal 8.0.0, whose `links = "signal"` admits one signal per build graph, and generate with ethos-zero 16.0.0 on protos and datom-codec 0.32.2. Only the store engine lags: flow and message pin sema-engine 0.16.0, whose read plans answer UnsupportedReadPlan and whose version stamp is not atomic; orchestrate pins 0.17.0, which executes the Filter read plan and lands an open as one write transaction; lojix pins 0.15.1 with signal-frame 0.3.1; sema-engine main is 0.18.0, adding Memorable. `nexus` 0.5.0 is a shared lifecycle-types library, not a template. messenger-clj is a Clojure CLI writing to Herdr directly, without a Nexus of its own; it reserves through the orchestrate CLI.

The orchestrate repository's `crates/orchestrate-nexus/tests/live_nexus.rs` runs a Nexus and both CLIs on temporary sockets with a fresh store. orchestrate-test (orchestrate c7c44cb3) and flow-test (flow 2fa51db8) pin the Nexus as a flake input and drive it in Nix scenarios: pure checks on a fresh or populated store, orchestrate-test's also resuming the store across the previous release 0.36.1, the live 0.35.0 and back (rollback), and gated semi-sandbox runners driving a cheapest-model Claude against it. These are the patterns for every sandbox check.

CriomOS-home branch `f1c841-orchestrate-0.37` (61abe3fb, on `3ec648-orchestrate-0.36`, which brings orchestrate 0.36.1 and wrappers that honour a caller's socket variables) moves Home to orchestrate 0.37.0; its generation is built, not switched and not merged.
